Privacy note

Plain-language summary of what this website does — and does not do — with your information.

English

Who we are. Dairy Park, Kolaghat – Jashar Road, Kolaghat, West Bengal 721134. Phone +91 99324 52124.

What this website does not do. There are no accounts, no login, no online payment, no delivery ordering and no checkout. We do not sell food or take bookings through this website; it exists to help you find us and call us.

The enquiry form. The contact page includes a small form that helps you note your name, phone number and question. The website has no server of its own and stores nothing: when you submit the form, your enquiry is written into a WhatsApp message addressed to the restaurant’s own WhatsApp number. WhatsApp opens on your own phone and nothing is transmitted until you tap Send there. If you never tap Send, we receive nothing.

What is stored on your device. Your language choice (English or Bengali) and, if you make one, your analytics choice. Both are saved in your browser's local storage. They are not sent anywhere and you can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

Analytics (only with your permission). If the restaurant has configured analytics and you tap “Allow analytics”, we may record anonymous events such as taps on “Call Now”, “Get Directions”, menu interest, promotion taps and language selection. This helps us understand which parts of the page are useful. No analytics script loads before you agree. We do not use advertising trackers.

Google Maps. The location section embeds a Google Map and links to our Google Maps listing. When the map loads, Google may receive your IP address and set cookies under Google's own privacy policy. If you prefer not to load it, simply use the “Get Directions” button or call us instead.

Links to other websites. This website links to Google Maps and to WhatsApp. When you continue to WhatsApp, WhatsApp’s own privacy policy applies to that chat and we do not control it. Your enquiry reaches us only because you send it from your own WhatsApp account, so we can see your WhatsApp name and number.

Children. This website is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and questions. If this website changes in a way that affects privacy, this note will be updated. For any question, call +91 99324 52124.

Last updated: 1 November 2026.

আপনার ডিভাইসে কী সংরক্ষিত হয়। আপনার ভাষার পছন্দ (ইংরেজি বা বাংলা) এবং অ্যানালিটিক্স নিয়ে আপনার সিদ্ধান্ত। দুটোই আপনার ব্রাউজারের লোকাল স্টোরেজে থাকে, কোথাও পাঠানো হয় না এবং ব্রাউজার সেটিংস থেকে যেকোনো সময় মুছে ফেলা যায়।

গুগল ম্যাপ। ঠিকানা অংশে গুগল ম্যাপ যুক্ত আছে এবং আমাদের গুগল ম্যাপ লিস্টিংয়ে লিংক আছে। ম্যাপ লোড হলে গুগল আপনার আইপি পেতে পারে এবং গুগলের নিজস্ব প্রাইভেসি নীতি অনুযায়ী কুকি রাখতে পারে। না চাইলে ম্যাপ না খুলে “পথনির্দেশ” বোতাম ব্যবহার করুন বা ফোন করুন।
