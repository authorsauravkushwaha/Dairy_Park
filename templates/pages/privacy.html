Plain-language summary of what this website does — and does not do — with your information.

English

Who we are. Dairy Park, Kolaghat – Jashar Road, Kolaghat, West Bengal 721134. Phone +91 99324 52124.

What this website does not do. There are no accounts, no login, no online payment, no delivery ordering and no checkout. We do not sell food or take bookings through this website; it exists to help you find us and call us.

The enquiry form. The contact page includes a small form that helps you note your name, phone number and question. Nothing you type is transmitted anywhere — the website has no messaging backend. The form simply points you to our phone number so you can call us yourself.

What is stored on your device. Your language choice (English or Bengali) and, if you make one, your analytics choice. Both are saved in your browser’s local storage. They are not sent anywhere and you can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

Analytics (only with your permission). If the restaurant has configured analytics and you tap “Allow analytics”, we may record anonymous events such as taps on “Call Now”, “Get Directions”, menu interest, promotion taps and language selection. This helps us understand which parts of the page are useful. No analytics script loads before you agree. We do not use advertising trackers.

Google Maps. The visit and contact pages embed a Google Map and link to our Google Maps listing. When the map loads, Google may receive your IP address and set cookies under Google’s own privacy policy. If you prefer not to load it, simply use the “Get Directions” button or call us instead.

Links to other websites. This website links to Google Maps. Those services have their own privacy policies which we do not control.

Children. This website is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and questions. If this website changes in a way that affects privacy, this note will be updated. For any question, call +91 99324 52124.
